PROTOTYPE #101 — card sheets, Figma rows 1 and 3

Sizes from the Figma export: 17 mm white marker zone, 13 mm marker, Geist Mono, a Tailwind 300 tint behind the label and (row 3) a 2 mm Tailwind 500 stripe where the tint starts. Printed cards are as long as their label needs. Handwritten cards are as long as the sample name needs, at about mm per handwritten letter. Markers are stand-ins (right size and layout, they don't read). Function cards don't print "call" or "p5.", only the name.
